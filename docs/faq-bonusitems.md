# What are Bonus Items?

Bonus Items provide additional rewards to all of your characters beyond what the Vanilla Slay the Spire II experience provides.

They're an optional feature, and you can customize what Bonus Items are available in the Multiworld! They're placed in the Multiworld pool _before_ any filler items are placed.

Like other rewards, Bonus Items are obtainable on every run - they aren't one-time consumables like Buffs or Traps.

However, they are usable by _all_ characters, rather than generated per-character like the other relic/gold/potion rewards.

> [!WARNING]
> Generation may fail if you have more Bonus Items defined than you have slots for filler items available! We recommend turning on **Floorsanity** when playing with Bonus Items on.
