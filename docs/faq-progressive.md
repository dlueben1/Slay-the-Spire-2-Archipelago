# What are Progressive Items?

Rather than receiving a specific item, like "Act 2 Ancient" or "Card Reward #7", items that have "Progressive" in the name provide the player the "next tier/step" of that item.

This allows for the collection of items to happen linearly, rather than out-of-order. For example, you wouldn't want to unlock Resting in Act 3 before you have it in Act 1.

For example, consider Ancient Unlocks in Slay the Spire II. By default, the Act 2 and Act 3 Ancients are locked, and you can't receive their rewards. Player B may have our "Progressive Ancient" in their world, while Player C may have our other "Progressive Ancient".

It doesn't matter which player gets "Progressive Ancient" first, the first time we receive it will unlock the Act 2 Ancient. Then, when we get the second one, we would receive the Act 3 Ancient.
