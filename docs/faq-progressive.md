# What are Progressive Items?

A progressive item unlocks the next step of something each time you receive one, so it doesn't matter which copy is found first. Your first Progressive Rest always unlocks resting in Act 1, the second in Act 2, and the third in Act 3.

Each character has its own copies of these progressive items, depending on your settings:

- **Progressive Ancient**: unlocks the next Ancient relic reward, the Act 2 Ancient and then the Act 3 Ancient. With Neow's Blessing on there are three, and the first unlocks Neow's reward. When the reward is offered depends on the Ancients setting below.
- **Progressive Rest** and **Progressive Smith** (Campfire Actions): three of each, unlocking resting or smithing at campfires one act at a time.
- **Progressive Shop Remove** (Shuffle Card Removal in Shop Sanity): three, unlocking card removal at shops one act at a time.
- **Progressive Starter Card** (Starting Equipment): two. You start without your special starter card; the first restores it and the second transforms it, as Archaic Tooth would (Bash becomes Break).
- **Progressive Starter Relic** (Starting Equipment): two. You start without your starter relic; the first restores it and the second refines it, as Touch of Orobas would (Burning Blood becomes Black Blood).
