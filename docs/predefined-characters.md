# Predefined modded characters

These modded characters have their own Archipelago IDs and appear under their display names in items and hints. In your YAML, enter either the display name or the full internal ID: in `modded_characters`, as an `advanced_characters` key, or as `unlocked_character`. Both are matched case-insensitively.

A generated roster can include all of them. They do not count towards the limit of 20 characters without a predefined name.
